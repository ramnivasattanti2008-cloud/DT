SUMMARY OF SCIENTIFIC FINDINGS & CONTRIBUTIONS:
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Identification of Four Prevalent Evaluation Shortcuts:
   - Pre-split TF-IDF and Chi-square feature leakage
   - Unpurged near-duplicate spam burst memorization (8% to 15%)
   - Look-ahead temporal leakage from randomly shuffled dated email streams
   - Blind evaluation at symmetric 0.50 cutoff ignoring false-alarm costs

2. New Evaluative Rubric (SERC, RCS, and CARI):
   - SERC: 10-item checklist evaluating data hygiene, split design, and cutoffs.
   - RCS: Reporting Completeness Score (0% to 100%).
   - CARI: Cost-Adjusted Reproducibility Index balancing F1, FPR, and RCS.

3. Systematic PRISMA 2020 Literature Audit:
   - Screened 642 articles down to 20 representative experimental studies (1999-2023).
   - High inter-rater reliability: Cohen's kappa = 0.8672, weighted kappa = 0.9071.
   - Exposed a statistically significant decline in reporting completeness post-2015 
     (RCS dropped from 77.14% down to 49.17%, p = 0.0023).

4. Controlled 5-Stage Empirical Ablation (6,000 Dated Email Benchmark):
   - Removing evaluation shortcuts slashes false positive rate from 3.33%-4.31% 
     down to 0.15%-0.29% (over tenfold reduction).
   - Eliminates 2.63 to 5.03 percentage points of artificial F1 optimism.
   - Elevates enterprise Total Cost Ratio (TCR at lambda=99) from 0.16-0.20 (<1.0 failure) 
     up to 1.58-2.48 (>1.0 viable utility), lifting CARI to 0.9130.
